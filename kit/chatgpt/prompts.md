# Prompts for Mahaam in ChatGPT

Replace the bracketed parts, e.g. `<project>`.

**Standup**
> Standup for <project> since yesterday: what's done, what's in review, what needs a decision, and what's next.

**Weekly client update**
> Write a client-facing weekly update for <project>. Leave out internal names, costs and markup.

**Triage**
> Go through the backlog of <project>. Flag duplicates, ask the one missing question on unclear
> issues, and suggest type and priority for the rest. Don't change statuses.

**File a bug from a message**
> File this as a bug in <project>, and check for duplicates first: <paste the report>

**Log time**
> Log 1h30 today on "<task>" in <project>: <what I did>.

**Timer**
> Start a timer on <task key>. / Stop my timer and tell me how long it ran.

**My week**
> Show my timesheet for this week and point out any day under 6 hours.

**Decisions waiting on me**
> List every issue waiting for approval across my projects, with each question in one line.

**AI cost (managers)**
> How much did AI cost on <project> this month, by model, and how much of it is still unbilled?
