# Age rating

## Apple (age rating questionnaire)

Every content category is **None**: cartoon/fantasy/realistic violence, sexual content, nudity,
profanity, alcohol/tobacco/drugs, mature themes, horror, medical info, gambling, contests.

- Unrestricted web access: **No.** Links open in the system browser, and the app has no browser of
  its own.
- User-generated content: **Yes**, but private. Issues, comments and requests are visible only to
  members of the same invited workspace, not to the public. Workspace admins can remove content and
  people.
- Messaging/chat between users: comments on issues, inside a workspace only.

Expected rating: **4+**. On the business-app questions, answer that it's made for businesses and teams.

## Google Play (IARC questionnaire)

- Category: **Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other**.
- Violence, sexuality, language, controlled substances, gambling: **No**.
- Users can interact or exchange content: **Yes**. Comments within an invited workspace, moderated
  by the workspace's admins, with no public sharing.
- Shares user location: **No**. Digital purchases: **No**.

Expected rating: **Everyone** (with the "Users Interact" notice). Target audience: **18 and over**
(a work tool), not designed for children.
