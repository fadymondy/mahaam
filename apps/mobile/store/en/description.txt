Mahaam keeps a team's projects, issues and time in one place — on the phone, the web, the desktop and in the browser — and gives your customers a portal of their own.

FOR YOUR TEAM
• Projects and issues: a board and a list, statuses, priorities, labels, checklists, comments and attachments.
• My work: everything assigned to you, grouped the way you work.
• Time tracking: start a timer on an issue in one tap, log time by hand, and see your week in a timesheet. The running timer follows you across your phone, computer and browser.
• Inbox: assignments, mentions and updates as they happen.
• Search across issues and projects.

FOR YOUR CUSTOMERS
• A portal to file requests, follow progress and read updates.
• Invoices from tracked time, ready to view on the phone.

BUILT FOR REAL WORK
• English and Arabic, with a full right-to-left layout, in light and dark.
• Two-step verification, Face ID / fingerprint unlock, and sign-in with Apple, Google or GitHub.
• Your data stays yours: delete your account from the app at any time.

Mahaam is for teams using a Mahaam workspace. Join yours with the invitation your admin sends, or create a workspace in the app.
